**The Homelab Postmortem** documents real, dated incidents from running actual
homelab hardware — Raspberry Pis, self-hosted services, the usual stack — and
the exact fixes that came out of them. No generic "best practices" content:
if a post is here, something specific broke first.

## How this is made

Posts are drafted with AI assistance, based on real incident logs, and
reviewed by a human before publishing. Every script referenced in a post is
tested against live hardware before it's included in the toolkit — not
generated from theory and hoped to work.

## In Japanese

## The toolkit

Scripts that come out of these postmortems are collected into a toolkit —
see the [toolkit page]({{ '/toolkit/' | relative_url }}) for what's in it.

## Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no
cookies, does not fingerprint your browser, and does not track you across
sites — so there's no consent banner, because there's nothing to consent to.
It tells me which posts people read. That's all it's for.
